WordMeaning here
DesignWritten as an idea or a definition. Not yet a running artifact on the cited tree.

Internally testedThe project’s own log reports a figure. Sample and protocol are stated when the source states them.
ManuscriptA paper or technical note written by the laboratory. Not accepted, unless a venue is named. None are named.
