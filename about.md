## Biography {#bio}

Jouly (马卓立 / ZHUOLI MA) designs and implements adaptive computing substrates end-to-end: runtime structure for persistent agents (**Valhalla**), auditable encryption research (**Yaksha Cipher**), and the Wave / Val-OS lineage that shares Fate-centered control ideas.

The laboratory identity is **Rogue Intelligence**. Public commercial emphasis for AI deployments is **agent reliability** — state, abstention, and recovery — not “AI cultivation platform” marketing.

Work spans Rust runtimes, protocolized Unique Proofs, trainability layers (structure session → Fate pretrain → controlled bake), cryptography research packs, and Wave-language / OS experiments. Theory work (asymmetric spiral / φ-algebra tooling such as S-wind) informs the lineage but is not the ValhallaBase sales lead.
