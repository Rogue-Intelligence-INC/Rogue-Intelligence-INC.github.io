S-WIND

A formal exploration system over the rational numbers. It keeps an auditable trace, and it is required to tell a discovery from a formula that merely passes through the points.

Research question

Given a short exact sequence, when is a closed form a discovery, when is it a known sequence, and when is it only an interpolation that any polynomial of high enough degree would satisfy?

Central idea

The manuscript states the criterion as a definition, not as a slogan. A result is a discovery only if it differs from a known authority, the algebraic check succeeds, and it is non-trivial.

Discovery = (Result ≠ Known authority) ∧ (Verified correct) ∧ (Non-trivial) Non-trivial means DoF = n − (d + 1) ≥ 2. For a polynomial of degree d there are d + 1 coefficients.

The same note gives the counterexample that makes the bound concrete. Eight points and a degree-7 polynomial give DoF = 8 − 8 = 0. The fit exists by the interpolation theorem. S-WIND must label it as interpolation, not as a law. The manuscript’s academic sentence is: fitting, including exact rational interpolation, is an auditable hypothesis generator. It is upgraded only after a pre-registered out-of-sample check inside a constrained pattern class.

v1.1.1 records a specific correction. Earlier versions treated out-of-sample verification as true without checking. The manuscript says 1.1.1 checks that an extended signal still satisfies the pattern, for example that a finite difference Δd stays constant.

Procedure, as written

The paper describes Fate as an explorer with a knowledge base, a list of discoveries, and a pool of signals. It does not publish a state-transition table beyond the steps below. This page does not add one.

  1. 01
    Receive

    A signal, as a sequence.

  2. 02
    Recognize

    Compare with the knowledge base. An OEIS match is recognition, labeled Known, and is not a discovery. The manuscript says OEIS is a refutation tool, not a permission to discover.

  3. 03
    Detect a class

    Voyager looks for constant difference, constant ratio, constant product, factorial, or a linear recurrence, and writes a derivation trace.

  4. 04
    Extend if the signal is short

    A self-loop lengthens the signal so that degrees of freedom can rise.

  5. 05
    Test outward, and stop at one failure

    Predict the next point. Consistency increases a success count. One failure returns the pattern to a descriptive model. The paper’s threshold sketch is min_points = pattern_degree + 2, with a computational cap of 20 points.

Labels in the manuscript include Known, TrivialInterpolation, DescriptiveOnly, CandidateLaw, StrongCandidate, GenuineDiscovery, and Unknown. GenuineDiscovery additionally asks for external validation. The fit levels run from trivial interpolant, to descriptive model, to constrained candidate, to emergent law.

EPR

Extended Precision Rational is the arithmetic. An EPR value is an arbitrary-precision numerator over an arbitrary-precision denominator, reduced by gcd, with no floating-point rounding. The engineering report says limbs are base 264 integers and that the type does not depend on an external big-integer crate. Addition, multiplication, division, and comparison are exact. The manuscript says this can represent 1000! precisely. Constructors such as from_i128 are described as conveniences, not as the mathematical bound.

Space, in this system, is the module that opens a container when a discovery does not fit an existing one, including a fractional dimension. Quad is a separate physics-simulation module in the engineering report. Those names are local to S-WIND.

What the manuscript reports

The abstract summarizes 22 experiments: 14 known sequences labeled recognized, 5 novel patterns labeled EmergentLaw with DoF at least 2, and 3 chaotic sequences rejected as InterpolantFit with DoF 0. This page has not re-run them. The full TeX is not posted here.

Two documents disagree on the size of the knowledge base. The manuscript says 78 theorems, 60 physical laws, and 203+ OEIS sequences. The 24 January 2026 engineering report draws a knowledge base of 199 sequences and a knowledge module of about 4,466 lines. Both figures are left as written. They are not averaged.

Limits

The local checkout used for this page contains the manuscript and the engineering report. It does not contain a readable S-WIND source tree. A public S-WIND repository was not in the organization listing reviewed while writing this site. Nothing here should be read as a re-implementation.

The paper is a manuscript, dated January 2026. It is not marked as accepted or peer reviewed. An archival notebook at Windohouse Lad discusses a spiral and φ-algebra lineage. It is not this manuscript.
