01 / Main project · Law-governed generation

Valhalla

Valhalla is the main project. It can cultivate its own body from an empty session: scheduling laws, formation roles, and a gate, with no language-model gradient step at the start. A language model, when one is attached later, only decodes.

Research question

Can a system cultivate an intelligence from an empty session — shared across text, speech, and image, able to abstain, and still readable after a language model is attached or swapped?

From an empty session

The 14 July 2026 design map names three structure layers. They start from an empty session. They do not begin by fine-tuning a pretrained language model.

  1. L1
    Zero-weight session

    Affinity is learned on the incubation snapshot. The map’s intended consequence is learning without a language-model gradient step.

  2. L2
    Fate pretraining

    Affinity epochs tighten the scheduler. The recorded trace is the hub-spread trajectory.

  3. L3
    Optional weight bake

    The fast scorecard reports a bake weight delta of about 298. That figure measures the write. It does not say a language model has been replaced.

Text, speech features, and image features then incubate Hub, Tile, and Stem under the scheduling law. Open answers are read from that body. A decoder can be attached afterwards. On 27 June 2026, a holdout session snapshot did not beat cold retrieval. That bound stays in force for that protocol.

Generation by law

Cultivation follows laws, in order. The implementation does not model neurons or copy a nervous system. The design map says not to call a designed piece discovered biology.

  1. 01
    Schedule

    quad_cycle rotates four named quadrants in a fixed order: BlackHole, MeshBrain, MultiNova, BaseForce. The order is a routing law.

  2. 02
    Form

    Hub routes. Tile completes a local pattern. Stem clusters. The design refuses a preset catalog of organs. Identity is a data signature. Coalescence is allowed by that rule, then measured.

  3. 03
    Gate

    The output gate emits or abstains when its algebra clears. Fate decides whether speaking is permitted. The gate does not sample the next token.

  4. 04
    Read

    Open questions use native retrieval over the structure. Multiple choice still uses a model head: on the cited 46-item scale, structure-only scoring was 35% and the model head was 63%.

The stack those laws sit in

The 14 July 2026 architecture note draws five layers around those laws. Ingress accepts text, speech features, image features, and appended session material. Fate schedules that material and can emit or abstain. The Triad body is Hub, Tile, and StemCell. Feedback returns into Fate. Readout is a native answer, with a language model drawn as a dashed, optional head.

  1. IngressOne session

    Modalities are pulses in the same session, not three stores fused at the end.

  2. Fatequad_cycle

    BlackHole, then MeshBrain, then MultiNova, then BaseForce.

  3. BodyHub, Tile, Stem

    Route, complete a local pattern, cluster toward organs. Fragments hold the compact structure.

  4. FeedbackContinuity

    Question-answer feedback and a persistent snapshot.

  5. ReadoutNative path

    Open questions can be answered from structure.

    Optional LM head. Default documented decoder: Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct, replaceable.

How a turn works

  1. 01
    Ingest

    Context lines and multimodal items build or extend an incubation snapshot. Appending changes the snapshot’s fingerprint.

  2. 02
    Route

    Production notes recommend fate_ingress_routing="quad_cycle" and fate question-answer feedback.

  3. 03
    Form structure

    Hub carries routing preference. Tile completes local pattern. StemCell clusters. The public formation record, protocol triad-structure-highlight-v1, reports 16 tiles, 108 stems, hub 104×4.

  4. 04
    Decode by question type

    Documented hybrid routing sends open questions to native retrieval and multiple choice to a model log-probability head. Structure-only multiple choice, on the cited 46-item scale, scored 35% against the model head’s 63%.

Names, and one collision

MeshBrain, Stem, and organ are role names inside the scheduling and formation laws. The protocol natural-nn-topology-v1 measures stem connectivity against an Erdős–Rényi null (σ ≈ 9.27 on that record). It is a graph measurement of the body those laws grew. “Organ” is not a catalog of brain parts: the design refuses preset labels, and same-type grouping is measured after formation.

BlackHole, MeshBrain, MultiNova, and BaseForce are the four Fate ingress quadrants, in that order. Wave-C uses the same four names for tile-memory pools. Those pools and this ingress cycle are separate implementations.

Limits

The public mirror explicitly does not claim a LoCoMo or LongMemEval win, or that Valhalla replaces a transformer.

On 27 June 2026, a progress snapshot reported that its holdout session snapshot did not exceed cold retrieval, and treated that as evidence against reading the runtime as a fine-tune substitute. July records add formation, topology, training, and unique-proof measurements. They are later, and they are different protocols.

Multiple choice still depends on the model head in the numbers cited above. The runtime and this documentation mirror are proprietary. Reproduction commands point at the licensed engineering checkout, not at this website.
