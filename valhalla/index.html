01 / Main project · Rules-first structural intelligence

Valhalla

Valhalla’s goal is a rules-first, self-forming neural network: a persistent body that grows its own Tile/Stem structure under explicit algebraic rules (the Fate criterion), stores every experience in four quad operators with exact accounting, and routes all later computation through the structure it formed. A language model may be attached later as a decoding head; it is not the object of study, and nothing in the measured core depends on one. Every governing rule is written down in the source, and the whole state can be watched from outside, frozen, and restored bit-exactly.

Positioning — built against three failure modes

Valhalla tests the alternative to frozen weights plus a transient context window: experience written into persistent structure, with computation thereafter routed through that structure. Three failure modes of the dominant stack define the problem, each addressed at the substrate level and each stated with its measured scope:

Research question

Deployed systems accumulate experience outside the computing substrate — in prompts, indices and rules — where it cannot change what is computed next. Can compact, stateful signal interactions modify what is computed next, and can reusable structure form from those interactions? Valhalla is the implemented test of that question. Scope statement: this is a research architecture with implemented mechanisms and pre-specified evidence. No claim of general native intelligence is made.

A signal is the tuple st = (source, intensity, direction, quality, logical time); complexity resides in state, not in signal size. Fate governs signal and state; the four quad operators (BlackHole, MeshBrain, MultiNova, BaseForce) store and maintain every fragment — eight accounts per fragment, Tile-side and Stem-side, exactly as designed; feedback from structure alters subsequent computation. Fate is the global measurement and scheduling layer; it does not veto body dynamics.

The stack

  1. IngressFate segmentation

    Facts, text, code, image, audio, MIDI, binary enter byte-wise through one entrance.

  2. FateCluster equation

    Judges convergence, bundling, segmentation. Adapts only on material it has itself judged. Live 50-round end-state: 0.7155x² − 1.8912x + 0.6700.

  3. QuadFour stores

    BlackHole, MeshBrain, MultiNova, BaseForce — each fragment stored in all four, maintained autonomously.

  4. BodyTile / Stem

    Populations split, merge and connect under field dynamics; connections bundled into nerves each cycle.

  5. EgressScored separately

    Storage retrieval · network multi-hop reachability · read-only interface returning stored originals verbatim. Never averaged into one score.

Mechanisms corrected into place (2–3 November 2026)

  1. M1
    Split dynamics, restored

    Energy > 1.5× population mean, at most 10 splits per cycle, flow rate ×0.1 — the original field rule, after measurement found and reverted a widening introduced during de-hardcoding.

  2. M2
    Fate bundling

    Distances that fit under the cluster equation’s convergence rule are bundled into one nerve. Graph cost fell by ≈99.8% at matched scale.

  3. M3
    Two-layer escape

    Storage layer: the BlackHole event-horizon law exempts escaping entries during autonomous maintenance. Graph layer: Fate opens long-range nerves to distant strong signals. The layers do not interfere.

  4. M4
    Equation adaptation, bound to Fate

    The equation adapts only inside passive reception, on ratios Fate just judged; judgement itself never modifies the equation.

White-box by design

Valhalla is not a black box with an explanation layer attached; the mechanism itself is the readable object. Five properties, all verifiable in the public code and logs:

  1. W1
    Written rules

    The governing criteria are explicit in the source: the Fate cluster equation, the split gate (energy > 1.5x mean, at most 10 per cycle), bundling under the equation's convergence rule, the quad escape law. Nothing that governs the measured core is a learned opaque weight.

  2. W2
    Self-reporting criterion

    The Fate equation prints itself at every checkpoint of every run (live 50-round end-state: 0.7155x2 - 1.8912x + 0.6700). You can read what judged your data, in the log, next to the result.

  3. W3
    External read-only monitor

    BodyMonitor (hub-f64 monitor.rs) sits outside the body and only watches: populations, connections, per-store ledgers with hashes and deltas, Fate tick and equation. The observer cannot write into the observed.

  4. W4
    Auditable accounting

    Every fragment is stored eight times by design (four quad operators x Tile/Stem), each store reporting entries / bytes / fingerprint hash per cycle. Storage claims check to the byte.

  5. W5
    Transportable state

    The full body (structure + quad storage + Fate) freezes, moves, and thaws with bit-identical scores (F = C), deterministically, across seeds on the reference setup.

Reproduce every number

Every Rust-core number on this page is generated by the one-click reproduction package (reproduce/ in the Valhalla repository): one command builds the suite, runs library tests (fate 7 / stem 4 / tile 8 / blackhole 3), the six check packages, the battery, the 15-seed incubation proof and the 50-round full flow, then verifies the headline readings against expected_results.txt. Any failure exits non-zero and names the item; nothing is retried and no criterion is adjusted after the fact.

The wider programme

Valhalla is the core of a small research programme. The other lines share its rules-first discipline but carry their own evidence — stated at the strength it actually has:

  • S-WIND — autonomous pattern/formula discovery from numerical data with exact rational arithmetic, and a discovery criterion that must reject zero-degree-of-freedom fits. Separate product repository.
  • Yaksha — parameterised symmetric encryption with a stateful AEAD protocol (Ocean wrapper, Dieharder record published with its failures). Component, in-workspace.
  • Wave — a language for intent and rule combinations, translated to x86-64, system calls, or a documented GPU mapping. Research line.
  • Windows shell — a currently usable enhancer for an instruction model that already exists; it does not cultivate a body. Sub-project.
  • Val-OS — operating-system research toward adaptive, self-organising system software (kernel/boot/desktop in the Valhalla repository). Research line.
  • Compression line — content-aware multi-system compression experiments, including high-entropy material. Experimental.
  • Papers programme — academic manuscripts maintained in-repo (algebraic determination of spacetime dimension; chemiosmotic origin-of-life framework). Manuscripts, not peer-reviewed results.
