Cryptography — Yaksha Cipher

Yaksha Cipher（夜叉密码） is a separate product line from ValhallaBase:

a Rust-centered symmetric encryption research stack emphasizing parameterized transforms, stateful AEAD, and — in the GPU-oriented line — auditable, hardware-adaptive throughput with reproducibility gates.
